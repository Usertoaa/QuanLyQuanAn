2. **Note Field Name**: `notes` → `note`
   - RestaurantAmenity model has a `note` field (singular)
   - Fixed in both saving and retrieval logic

3. **Context Data Retrieval**:

   - `restaurant.amenities.values_list('category_id', flat=True)` ✅
   - `amenity.note` instead of `amenity.notes` ✅

### **How to Use:**

1. Go to `/my-admin/restaurants/edit/{id}/`
2. Scroll to "Tiện Ích Quán" section
3. Check/uncheck amenities as needed
4. Optionally add notes for each amenity
5. Save changes
6. Amenities will display on restaurant detail page with icons and notes
